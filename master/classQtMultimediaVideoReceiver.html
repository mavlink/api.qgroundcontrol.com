Detailed Description

A network video stream played by Qt Multimedia's QMediaPlayer (FFmpeg): the receiver of builds without GStreamer, and of VideoSettings::videoBackend's Qt Multimedia choice. Plays RTSP, MPEG-TS over UDP or TCP, and H.264 or H.265 RTP over UDP with payload type 96, which an SDP description tells FFmpeg about. Records by encoding the frames it displays anew (QtVideoRecorder): only while an output displays the stream, and after a reconnect in the same file.

Streaming once the first frame arrives; decoding while frames reach the output. A stream that errors, ends, or shows no frame for the config's timeout while displaying (5 s more for the first, while FFmpeg probes the stream) has failed: its StreamSupervisor reconnects it after 1 s, doubling up to 30 s, while autoReconnect is on, and it stops with errorOccurred() otherwise.

◆ playerSource()

std::optional< QtMultimediaVideoReceiver::Source > QtMultimediaVideoReceiver::playerSource ( const QString & uri)
static

The source for uri, one of VideoStreamResolver's: rtsp(s):// and tcp:// as they are, rtspt:// as rtsp:// over TCP, as GStreamer plays it, mpegts:// as udp://, and udp:// (H.264) or udp265:// (H.265) RTP as an SDP description. Other URLs play as they are; nullopt for an invalid one.
