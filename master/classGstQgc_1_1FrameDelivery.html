Detailed Description

qgcqvideosink's latest-frame-only hand-off to a QVideoSink, on the QVideoSink's thread.

At most one invoke is queued to the QVideoSink, and it takes whatever frame is newest when it runs, so a stalled GUI thread never piles up frames, or the decoder buffers they pin. Thread-safe.

◆ setSink()

void GstQgc::FrameDelivery::setSink ( QVideoSink * sink)

Delivers to sink from now on; nullptr stops delivering. A frame bound for the previous sink is dropped, and an invoke still queued to it finds nothing. The controller clears the sink here from the QVideoSink's destroyed handler, which can't interleave with a deliver(): an invoke deliver() queued meanwhile is purged by ~QObject.
