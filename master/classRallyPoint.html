◆ useTerrainAltitude()

void RallyPoint::useTerrainAltitude ( bool  switchToAbsoluteOnFailure = false)

Asks for the terrain elevation at the point. On success the point switches to the Absolute frame with that elevation as altitude and follows the terrain while it moves, until the altitude is edited. On failure the altitude is left unchanged and the frame too, unless switchToAbsoluteOnFailure is set. That lets the user enter an AMSL altitude when terrain data is not available.
