Detailed Description

The display branch behind GstVideoReceiver's tee: leaky queue ! valve [! decodebin3 ! video sink].

Holds the caller's video sink until the source streams, then inserts decodebin3 and links its pad to the sink. The caller keeps its own ref on the sink; the branch takes one more, and the pipeline its own while the sink is inside. Decoding is reported from the first frame that reaches the sink; taking the sink out again clears the frame its QVideoSink displays. Also keeps the name of the decoder decodebin3 plugged, which the receiver exposes. Reports through the receiver's signals.

Threading: the receiver's worker, except the sink probe and the decoder-added callback (streaming threads) and onBusCapsInfo() (whichever thread posts). decoderName() is thread-safe.
