◆ msg_id

uint16_t sbf_buf_t::msg_id

The CRC field is the 16-bit CRC of all the bytes in an SBF block from and including the ID field to the last byte of the block. The generator polynomial for this CRC is the so-called CRC-CCITT polynomial: x 16 + x 12 + x 5 + x 0 . The CRC is computed in the forward direction using a seed of 0, no reverse and no final XOR.

◆ WNc

uint16_t sbf_buf_t::WNc

The GPS week number associated with the TOW. WNc is a continuous weekcount (hence the "c"). It is not affected by GPS week roll overs, which occur every 1024 weeks. By definition of the Galileo system time, WNc is also the Galileo week number + 1024.
