Detailed Description

Receive-only GNSS stream from UDP datagrams, for receivers that QGroundControl does not configure. The first sender is selected; another sender replaces it only after the selected one stays silent for PEER_IDLE_TIMEOUT. Datagrams cannot be held back, so input beyond READ_BUFFER_BYTES drops the oldest buffered bytes, with a warning; the decoders' checksums reject the frames a drop cuts. Owns its socket on the receiver worker.
