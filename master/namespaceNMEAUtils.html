QByteArray NMEAUtils::makeGGA ( const NMEA::GGA &  fix,
const QTime &  utc 
)

Build GPGGA from explicit fix fields and UTC, truncated to whole seconds. NaN altitude/geoid separation/HDOP and absent satellite count produce empty fields, not defaults. Returns empty for invalid UTC, out-of-range/nonfinite coordinates, infinite measurements, negative HDOP, or quality above the protocol maximum. Altitude is MSL; geoid separation is signed.
