Detailed Description

Also logs the receiver's worker, its PipelineSession with the branches, and PipelineDotDumper.

The GStreamer VideoReceiver.

This GUI-thread facade owns the display sink, built from VideoSettings once there is an output to show and rebuilt by restart(), and binds it to the output's QVideoSink through a QGCQVideoSinkController. After every request it hands the whole GstReceiverRequest to a GstReceiverWorker on a thread of its own, which runs the pipeline (one PipelineSession per connection) and steers it towards what was asked for: reconnecting with backoff, replacing a failed decoder, resuming a recording in a new part. The worker reports its GstReceiverStatus and events back through queued signals, so every signal is emitted on the GUI thread.
