Detailed Description

u-blox receiver configuration as data: the CFG-VALSET batches of protocol 27 and later, the CFG-PRT, CFG-RATE, CFG-NAV5, CFG-MSG and CFG-TMODE3 commands of older receivers, and the timeouts and values they use. Every VALSET batch is one message on the wire, so its boundaries are part of the configuration.
