Detailed Description

Video

Network video streams and USB cameras: their display, recording and statistics. Three receivers play video behind one interface, VideoReceiver: GStreamer's (GstVideoReceiver), Qt Multimedia's (QtMultimediaVideoReceiver) and the USB camera's (UVCCameraController).

This page is a map of the folders. GStreamer/README.md is the guide to the GStreamer backend, and also says how the sources and tiles of VideoManager behave.

Where to start reading

  1. VideoManager.h: its class comment says what the video sources are.
  2. Streams/VideoStreamController.h: one source.
  3. Receiver/VideoReceiver.h: what a source asks of its receiver, whichever backend plays it.
  4. One receiver. For GStreamer: GStreamer/GStreamerVideoBackend.h, then GStreamer/README.md.

Folders

  • This folder: VideoManager, the QML facade of the sources (the primary stream, the thermal stream, the USB camera and the streams in tiles), and VideoTelemetryOverlay, the telemetry text shown over the video.
  • Streams/: VideoStreamController, one for each source, and VideoStreamResolver, which turns the video settings and a camera's advertised stream into a VideoReceiver::Config.
  • Recording/: what is recorded and where. VideoRecordingService names the files, starts and stops the sources' recordings and keeps the storage limit; SubtitleWriter writes the telemetry subtitles beside a recording.
  • Receiver/: the receiver contract and what its implementations share: VideoReceiver, VideoBackend, the reconnect policy (StreamSupervisor), the files of a recording (RecordingPlan), screenshots, stream statistics and URI schemes. No receiver that plays anything is here: those are in the next three folders.
  • GStreamer/: the GStreamer backend, listed below.
  • QtMultimedia/: QtMultimediaVideoReceiver, a stream played by QMediaPlayer: in builds without GStreamer, by choice (VideoSettings::videoBackend), or once GStreamer failed to start. Also QtVideoRecorder, the QMediaRecorder that it and the USB camera record with, and SharedVideoFrame.
  • UVC/: the USB camera: UVCCameraController (a QCamera as a receiver), UVCDevices and UVCStreamMonitor.
  • Qml/: the QGroundControl.VideoManager QML module: VideoView, the tiles, the video outputs, and the statistics and settings groups. src/FlyView, src/FlightMap/Widgets, src/Toolbar and the video settings page import it.

Inside GStreamer/:

  • GStreamerVideoBackend: the VideoBackend, the only way the app reaches GStreamer.
  • Runtime/: what must be up before a pipeline runs (initialization, environment, logging, decoder ranks), and the helpers the other folders use.
  • Source/: the source bins, from a stream's URI to its parsed stream (GstSourceFactory).
  • Pipeline/: the receiver, its worker, and a PipelineSession with a DecodingBranch and a RecordingBranch.
  • Sink/: the display sink: QGCQVideoSinkController and the gstqgc GStreamer plugin (Sink/gstqgc/).
  • HwBuffers/: how a decoded frame becomes a QVideoFrame without a copy: the HwBuffers facade, a folder for each GPU path, and common/, which the paths share.
  • SelfTest/: the boot self-test and the encoded samples it decodes and records (Media/).
  • Platform/: two C files for Android and iOS.

Video code outside this folder: src/Settings/VideoSettings, src/Camera/QGCVideoStreamInfo (a stream a camera advertises), src/AnalyzeView/VideoRecordings (the page that plays recordings) and src/Comms/MockLink/MockVideoStreamServer (MockLink's test stream).

The rules the folders keep

  • VideoSettings is read by VideoManager.cc, Streams/ and Recording/VideoRecordingService.cc only, and the vehicle and its cameras are known to this folder, Streams/ and Recording/TelemetryBarValues.cc only. A receiver gets a VideoReceiver::Config and a VideoReceiver::DisplayConfig.
  • Receiver/ names a backend in one place, VideoBackend::instance().
  • The backends include each other in two places: GStreamerVideoBackend makes QtMultimediaVideoReceivers once GStreamer failed to start, and UVC/ records with QtMultimedia/QtVideoRecorder.
  • Inside GStreamer/ the includes point one way: GStreamerVideoBackend and SelfTest/, then Pipeline/, then Source/, then Runtime/.
  • HwBuffers/ and Sink/gstqgc/ are the static library QGCGStreamer, which is not a unity build. Of the folders above they include one header, Runtime/GstScoped.h. Everything else is compiled into the application, which CI builds as unity units: a file-local name must not repeat in another .cc of the application.
  • With QGC_ENABLE_GST_VIDEOSTREAMING off, nothing of GStreamer/ is built, and streams play through QtMultimedia/.

A frame, from the network to the screen

  1. Streams/VideoStreamResolver makes a VideoReceiver::Config from the video settings, or from the stream a camera of the vehicle advertises.
  2. Streams/VideoStreamController hands it to its receiver: setConfig(), setActive(), setVideoOutput().
  3. GStreamer/Pipeline/GstVideoReceiver, on the GUI thread, passes each request to its GstReceiverWorker, which runs one PipelineSession on a thread of its own.
  4. GStreamer/Source/GstSourceFactory builds the source bin for the URI. It feeds the session's tee.
  5. GStreamer/Pipeline/DecodingBranch decodes with decodebin3, which plugs the decoder that GStreamer/Runtime/GStreamerDecoderRanks ranked first.
  6. GStreamer/Sink/gstqgc/gstqgcqvideosink makes a QVideoFrame of the decoded frame (GstQgcFrameMap): through a GPU path of GStreamer/HwBuffers/, or on the CPU (HwBuffers/common/CpuVideoFramePool).
  7. GStreamer/Sink/gstqgc/GstQgcFrameDelivery gives the latest frame to the QVideoSink that GStreamer/Sink/QGCQVideoSinkController bound.
  8. Qml/VideoStreamOutput.qml shows it, inside VideoView.qml or VideoTile.qml.

The other receivers take fewer steps. QtMultimedia/QtMultimediaVideoReceiver has a QMediaPlayer play into the QVideoSink. UVC/UVCCameraController puts a QCamera in a capture session, which Qml/UVCVideoOutput.qml shows.

Each layer counts for itself (GStreamer/Pipeline/PipelineStatsProbe, GStreamer/Source/GstRtpPacketProbe, GStreamer/Sink/gstqgc/GstQgcSinkCounters, GStreamer/HwBuffers/common/GstHwPathTelemetry, UVC/UVCStreamMonitor), and Receiver/VideoStreamStatsMeter and Receiver/VideoStreamStatsSampler make the VideoStreamStats of the counters that Qml/VideoStreamStatsGroup.qml shows.

A recording

  1. VideoManager::startRecording() calls Recording/VideoRecordingService, which picks the file name, applies the storage limit and calls VideoStreamController::startRecording() for the main picture and the thermal stream.
  2. GStreamer: GstReceiverWorker follows a Receiver/RecordingPlan, which names the parts a recording continues in, and GStreamer/Pipeline/RecordingBranch writes each file (parser, muxer, file sink).
  3. Qt Multimedia and the USB camera: QtMultimedia/QtVideoRecorder encodes the stream's decoded frames, or the camera's capture session.
  4. recordingStarted(file) starts Recording/SubtitleWriter for that file, and recordingFinished() stops it.
  5. src/AnalyzeView/VideoRecordings plays the recordings with their subtitles.

Where settings come in

VideoManager::_connectSettings() connects the video settings to the sources, and its comments list every setting:

  • The video source, its URLs, the latency and reconnect settings, and which tiles there are: the sources resolve their config again, and a receiver restarts for a changed config.
  • videoConversionElement, disablePixelAspectRatio, forceCpuVideoPath: the sources restart, which rebuilds the display sink.
  • forceVideoDecoder: the decoder ranks are applied again, and the sources restart.
  • videoBackend: each stream gets a new receiver.
  • The USB camera's settings (uvcCameraFormat, uvcZoom and the like) go to UVCCameraController. The recording format and the storage limit are read when a recording starts.

What is platform-specific

  • GStreamer/HwBuffers/: d3d/ is for Windows, apple/ for macOS and iOS, dmabuf/ for Linux, gl/ for Linux and Android; cuda/ (Linux), android/ and vulkan/ are built only on request. GStreamer/HwBuffers/CMakeLists.txt has the table of the paths with their conditions.
  • GStreamer/Platform/, which cmake/GStreamer/platform/Android.cmake and cmake/platform/Apple.cmake compile.
  • cmake/GStreamer/: how each platform's GStreamer SDK is found, downloaded and installed.
  • #if blocks in GStreamer/Runtime/GStreamerEnvironment.cc (the layout of each platform's install) and UVC/UVCCameraController.cc.

Tests

test/VideoManager has the same folders: the tests of Receiver/ are in test/VideoManager/Receiver/, those of GStreamer/Source/ in test/VideoManager/GStreamer/Source/. Qml/ has no folder there: VideoManagerTest tests the QML module. GStreamer/README.md lists the GStreamer classes and says how to run them.
