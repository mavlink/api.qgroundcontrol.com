Detailed Description

Single source of truth linking a GStreamer video format to its Qt pixel format and (optionally) its negotiation caps token. Every advertised caps format list (GstQgcCaps) and toQtPixelFormat() (GstQgcFrameMap) derive from this table, so a format can never be advertised without a Qt mapping, nor mapped-and-renderable without being offered.
