Detailed Description

Scoped owners for transfer-full GStreamer returns so refs can't leak on early return. Use the adopt* helpers at the transfer-full call boundary; .get() yields a non-owning view. Floating refs (e.g. gst_element_factory_make) are consumed by gst_bin_add/gst_element_add_pad even when the call fails, so release() the owner into the call, or sink them at creation with makeElement().

◆ serializeUdpSrcUnlock()

void GStreamer::serializeUdpSrcUnlock ( )

Before gst-plugins-good 1.24.10, udpsrc's unlock_stop() replaces its GCancellable on the streaming thread (basesrc defers it to the first create() after PLAYING) while unlock() cancels it from the thread leaving PLAYING. A pipeline stopped right after it started then cancels a freed GCancellable and corrupts the heap. On those versions this wraps the two udpsrc class methods to serialize them, as the upstream fix does (gstreamer!7897); on newer ones, or without udpsrc, it does nothing. Runs once per process, after gst_init().
