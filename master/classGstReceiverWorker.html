Detailed Description

GstVideoReceiver's worker, on a thread of its own: holds what the receiver was asked for (active, config, video sink, recording) and keeps a PipelineSession in line with it, reconnecting with backoff after a failure.

Reports back through its signals only, so it can outlive a receiver that stopped waiting for it.
