Detailed Description

GstVideoReceiver's worker, on a thread of its own: steers a PipelineSession towards the receiver's latest GstReceiverRequest (active, config, video sink, restarts, recording), reconnecting after a failure as its StreamSupervisor decides.

Reports back through its signals only, so it can outlive a receiver that stopped waiting for it.
