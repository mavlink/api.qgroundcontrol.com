Detailed Description

Hosts a native receiver family and adapts its decoded events to public reports. GPSType::automatic detects the family (GPSReceiverDetector) on every configure(), then fits the request to it: consent to persistent changes lapses where the family has none, a compact-observation request lapses with a warning where the family has no compact option, and an unsupported base mode fails, naming the family.
