Detailed Description

Measures the USB camera on display, from the frames its capture session hands the video sink, about once a second while frames arrive. UVCCameraController attaches its camera and the sink of the registered VideoOutput.

stats fills valid, fps, codec (the camera's capture format, e.g. "MJPEG" or "YUYV"), gpuPath ("CPU" for frames in memory, "GPU" for textures) and uptimeMs; the network stream fields keep their defaults.
