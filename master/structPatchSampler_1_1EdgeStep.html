Detailed Description

Largest rendered one-cell step off the patch edge not explained by the patch's own data, at a boundary vertex resolved from a different-zoom tile (typically a neighbor's). The quarter of each edge next to a corner is skipped: a corner resolved to other data leaves an expected one-cell notch there that no interior blend can remove.
