Detailed Description

Standard NMEA navigation and satellite epochs plus RTCM3 pass-through, composed by the ASCII receiver families. Binary RTCM payloads never enter the line parser. A family hands each frame, a line or a checked sentence, to onFrame() with its vendor handling, which shares the working position through position().
