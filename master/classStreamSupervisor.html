Detailed Description

The reconnect policy both receivers share: the phase a stream is in, when a failed one connects again, and when it gives up.

The receiver runs the sessions (a GStreamer pipeline, a QMediaPlayer) and tells the supervisor what becomes of them; the supervisor reports the VideoReceiver::State that follows and signals reconnectDue() once a backoff has passed. A failure reconnects after 1 s, doubling up to 30 s, unless it is permanent or autoReconnect is off: then the stream halts until startOver(). A session that streamed had recovered, so the backoff starts over after it fails.

Threading: the receiver's thread.
