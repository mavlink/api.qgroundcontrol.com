◆ kMaxPinnedFrames

int CpuVideoFramePool::kMaxPinnedFrames = 4
staticconstexpr

Upper bound on decoder buffers one sink holds downstream at once, zero-copy CPU and GPU frames alike: the frame being mapped in show_frame, one pending GUI delivery (latest-frame-only), the frame Qt displays, and the previous frame, whose texture bundle keeps its sample until Qt reuses that in-flight slot (QRhi FramesInFlight is 2 on the common backends).
