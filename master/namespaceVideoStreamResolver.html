Maps the video settings and a camera's advertised stream to what a stream source's VideoReceiver plays.

Enumerator
Primary 

The stream to display: a vehicle's current camera's current one, else the one the settings configure.

Thermal 

The thermal stream a vehicle's current camera advertises.

Camera 

The local camera: the one the video source setting names or, beside a stream, the one for a tile.

Tile 

Another stream a vehicle's cameras advertise, shown in a tile.

What the source of role plays: the stream streamInfo advertises, or else, for the primary role only, the manually configured one. nullopt when there is none, as for the camera role, which plays no stream. Reads only its arguments: a camera's stream never changes the user's settings.
