Detailed Description

u-blox receivers over UBX: CFG-VALSET configuration from protocol 27, CFG-MSG and CFG-TMODE3 before it.

UBX payload records with the fields QGC reads or writes. Records of decoded messages carry their message ID; bytes without a field are skipped on decode and zero on encode.
