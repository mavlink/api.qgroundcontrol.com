◆ GpuBinMode

enum class HwBuffers::GpuBinMode
strong

Head of qgcvideosinkbin's GPU branch. Linux builds with both gst-gl and DMABuf compile GlUpload and DirectDmaBuf and pick one at runtime; every other build compiles exactly one.

Enumerator
Passthrough 

Every compiled native memory straight into qgcqvideosink (Windows, Apple, ...).

GlUpload 

glupload → glcolorconvert → GLMemory 2D; gst-gl imports LINEAR and vendor-tiled DMABuf.

DirectDmaBuf 

memory:DMABuf and DMA_DRM into QGC's own importer (LINEAR only), skipping gst-gl's copy.
