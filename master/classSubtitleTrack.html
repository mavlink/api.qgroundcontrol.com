Detailed Description

A recording's telemetry subtitle file, read for display over the video: the events shown at the playback position, in the file's own coordinates (playResX × playResY).

Reads the Advanced SubStation Alpha subset SubtitleWriter writes: the Default style's font size, alignment and margins, and Dialogue events with {\anN} and {\pos(x,y)} overrides and \N line breaks. Other override tags are dropped.
