Detailed Description

The USB (UVC) camera VideoManager selected: owns its QCamera and capture session, measures its frames, takes its photos and records it. The user hears of camera errors and of the camera being unplugged.

The camera is tracked by its device id, so unplugging it is noticed even while another camera of the same name stays attached; the controller then runs that one. After an error the camera is recreated up to kMaxRetries times.

The camera runs while one is selected and a QML VideoOutput is registered: registerVideoOutput() once it is created, unregisterVideoOutput() when it is destroyed (see UVCVideoOutput.qml).
