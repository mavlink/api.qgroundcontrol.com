Detailed Description

A USB (UVC) camera as a VideoReceiver: the camera the config's URI names by its description, which is its video source name. Owns its QCamera and capture session, measures its frames, takes its photos and records it. The user hears of camera errors and of the camera being unplugged.

The camera is tracked by its device id, so unplugging it is noticed even while another camera of the same name stays attached; the controller then runs that one. After an error the camera is recreated up to kMaxRetries times.

The camera runs while the receiver is active and has a VideoOutput: a UVCVideoOutput.qml, which registers itself once it is created and turns and mirrors the picture as rotation and mirrored say, with its orientation and mirrored properties. Stopped until then, streaming from the camera's first frame, and reconnecting while it waits to retry after an error.
