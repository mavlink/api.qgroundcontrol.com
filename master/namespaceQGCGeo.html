◆ convertDMSToDegrees()

double QGCGeo::convertDMSToDegrees ( const QString & text,
bool isLatitude,
bool * ok = nullptr )

Parse a latitude or longitude typed in any common notation. Accepts degrees-minutes-seconds ("34 44 17.6 N", "34°44'17.6\"N"), degrees-decimal-minutes ("34 44.293 N") and decimal degrees ("34.7382", "-86.6017"). The hemisphere letter (N/S for latitude, E/W for longitude, either case) may lead or trail; without one a leading '-' means S/W.

Parameters
textText to parse.
isLatitudeTrue for latitude (N/S, +/-90), false for longitude (E/W, +/-180).
[out]okSet to false for malformed input, a hemisphere letter of the other axis, '-' together with a hemisphere letter, minutes/seconds >= 60, or an out of range value.
Returns
Signed decimal degrees, or 0 on failure.
