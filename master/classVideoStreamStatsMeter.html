Detailed Description

Turns a stream's cumulative counters, sampled about once a second, into the rates and totals of VideoStreamStats.

Counters count from the start of the stream. A counter below its previous value (the element counting it was replaced) has restarted from zero, so totals keep growing and rates never go negative.

fps and bitrateKbps average over the last kRateWindowIntervals intervals: frames and packets arrive in bursts, so a one-second count of a steady 15 fps stream reads anywhere from 12 to 18. droppedFps and lossPercent cover the last interval only, as they tell whether the stream degrades now.
