Detailed Description

The frame a buffer holds and the picture in it, as upstream's sinks read them (glimagesink, waylandsink): the frame is as large as the buffer's GstVideoMeta, which a decoder pads beyond the caps (1088 rows for 1080) or leaves uncropped, and the picture is its GstVideoCropMeta, else the caps' size at the frame's origin. A GstVideoAffineTransformationMeta of scales, translations and flips, as Android's amcvideodec attaches for its SurfaceTexture's crop and orientation, narrows the picture to the part it shows and tells which way to flip it. That part is the whole picture, squeezed with the decoder's padding into a texture of the caps' size (1080 rows and 8 of padding into 1080, of which 1072 show), so the picture keeps the size it had: pictureSize.
