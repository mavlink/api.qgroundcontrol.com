Detailed Description

What configuration may rely on for a board. The MON-VER hardware version selects a generation; the firmware and module strings then refine u-blox 9 to F9P and u-blox 10 to DAN-F10N.

uint16_t UBX::ReceiverProfile::measurementPeriodMs = 100

Measurement period (CFG-RATE-MEAS) until RTCM output starts. F9P computes 5 Hz with RTK and four constellations on firmware 1.50 and later; X20 could run at 25 Hz, but drops out at 115200 baud with RTK.
