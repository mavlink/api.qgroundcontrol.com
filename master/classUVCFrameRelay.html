Detailed Description

Hands the USB camera's frames on to the VideoOutput's sink, turned and mirrored as the rotation and mirror settings say. Turning the frames instead of the VideoOutput (VideoOutput.orientation) keeps upright what Qt draws over them: the telemetry overlay, the output's subtitle.

Frames arrive on the camera's thread; the latest is handed on on this object's thread, older ones it replaced dropped, as the display would.

A frame showing frame's picture, turned by rotation and mirrored by mirrored on top of frame's own rotation and mirroring. It shares frame's buffer but not its metadata: copies of a QVideoFrame share their rotation, and the recorder and image capture hold copies of the camera's frames.
