Detailed Description

What qgcqvideosink keeps about the path its frames take through one caps epoch, from set_caps to the next set_caps or stop: the GPU path it resolved, and what is once per epoch, a stream demotion and the tiled-drop warning. Also decides what becomes of a frame that fails to map: dropped, or after a sustained run of failures, an error that ends the stream.

Threading: the sink's streaming thread; GstBaseSink serializes set_caps, show_frame and stop.
