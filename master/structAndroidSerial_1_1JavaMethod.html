Detailed Description

template<typename Ret, typename... Args>
struct AndroidSerial::JavaMethod< Ret, Args >

A static method of QGCUsbSerialManager.java, declared by the JNI types of what it returns and of what it takes. JNI takes the arguments of a call as C varargs, so arguments that disagree with the signature the method was found under would compile, and corrupt the call. Here the signature is derived from the declared types, and a call compiles only with arguments of exactly those types.
