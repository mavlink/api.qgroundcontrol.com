Detailed Description

Holds one ring slot, plus refs on its COM objects, from acquire() until the last holder (the D3D12 video buffer and the frame bundle QRhi samples) drops it; another stream or a later frame can't reset its allocator/list or overwrite its resource meanwhile. Its copy has completed by the time the first holder exists (the resolve fence-waits), so the next acquirer may Reset() the allocator immediately.
