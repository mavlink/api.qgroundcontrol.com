Detailed Description

One receiver pipeline: source ! tee, with the display and recording branches behind the tee.

Lives on the receiver's worker thread and owns everything about its pipeline: the elements, the bus, the probes and the branches. Streaming threads hand their work to the session as queued calls, so once shutdown() has stopped the pipeline and detached the bus, deleting the session drops whatever they left behind.

Bus handling, through a GstBusPump: a sync handler does only what can't wait (GPU context requests, stream selection, and closing the valve in front of a failing branch); everything else is popped on the worker, where the bus's poll fd wakes it up.

The session recovers its branches from their errors itself: a failing decoder is replaced, a failing recorder ends the recording. A stalled decoder is the listener's to notice, and to replace through restartDecoder(). Failures of the stream as a whole go to the Listener, which decides whether to reconnect.

◆ shutdown()

void PipelineSession::shutdown ( )

Drains an open recording into its file (at most RecordingBranch::kFinalizeTimeout), stops the pipeline and detaches the bus: nothing the pipeline does reaches the session afterwards. Reports the end of decoding and of the recording to the listener. Idempotent; the destructor calls it too.
