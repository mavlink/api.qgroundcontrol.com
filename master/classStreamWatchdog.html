Detailed Description

Health of one receiver's stream: notices when frames stop arriving, and paces the reconnects after a failure.

Times are monotonic milliseconds from now(), passed in so the policy can be checked without waiting on a clock. Threading: noteSourceFrame() and noteSinkFrame() run on streaming threads; everything else on the receiver's worker.
