Detailed Description

One video stream's health, as its VideoReceiver (UVCStreamMonitor for a USB camera) measured it last: about once a second while it streams.

A field that doesn't apply to the stream is -1, which tells it apart from a measured zero: the RTP fields for a stream without an RTP jitter buffer (MPEG-TS, or UDP RTP in low-latency mode), latencyMs while the pipeline can't tell its latency, keyframeIntervalMs until two keyframes arrived and the recording fields while not recording. Strings are empty until known.
