Detailed Description

QML facade of the video sources, each a VideoStreamController: the primary stream, the thermal stream, the USB camera, whose receiver is uvcCamera, and the streams shown in tiles. One of the primary stream and the camera is the main picture: the camera while the video source setting names one and no vehicle camera advertises a stream. decoding and aspectRatio forward to the primary stream, thermalAspectRatio to the thermal stream, and recording covers the main picture and the thermal stream. The other properties, such as hasVideo and isUvc, are the manager's own state.

The tiles show what else there is to see, up to VideoSettings::maxVideoTiles of them, and no more than the row of tiles has room for (tileRoom): the USB camera VideoSettings::uvcTileCamera names, while a stream is the video source, then the other streams of the active vehicle's current camera, then its other cameras' streams. Each stream in a tile has a source of its own, which plays that stream for as long as it is shown. When the vehicle's current stream becomes one that plays in a tile, that source becomes the primary stream and the former one takes a tile: neither stream restarts. The camera selected in its tile (selectSource()) is the main picture as well, with the primary stream in a tile, until a stream is selected.

The source of the vehicle's current stream, or of the stream the settings configure. Never null.

Every source, for what treats them alike: the primary stream, the thermal stream, the USB camera, then the streams in tiles, in the tiles' order. A source falls short (VideoStreamStats::degraded()) of the frame rate its camera is set up for: the one a stream's camera advertises, while the stream is decoded, or the USB camera's own. A USB camera plays, as the main picture (isUvc) or in a tile. The source whose figures stand for the video's: the first one that is measured, those of the main picture before those in tiles, which puts the primary stream before the thermal stream; the USB camera runs in the primary stream's place. The primary stream while none is measured. Never null. How many sources are shown in tiles. How many tiles the row of tiles has room for, which it tells while it is shown: no more sources than that play in tiles, whatever VideoSettings::maxVideoTiles allows. -1 until a row tells. The telemetry VideoView shows over the primary stream or the USB camera: empty while VideoSettings::showTelemetryOverlay is off.
