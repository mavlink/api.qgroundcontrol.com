Detailed Description

QML facade of the video streams, each a VideoStreamController (primary, thermal). decoding and aspectRatio forward to the primary stream, thermalAspectRatio to the thermal stream, and recording covers both streams. uvcCamera runs the USB camera on display. The other properties, such as hasVideo and isUvc, are the manager's own state.
