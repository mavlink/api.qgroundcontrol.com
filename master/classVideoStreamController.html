Detailed Description

One video source: the primary stream, the thermal stream, the local camera or a stream shown in a tile. Resolves the source's config from the video settings and the vehicle camera's advertised stream, drives the source's VideoReceiver, and connects the receiver to the QML VideoOutput that displays it.

A stream's receiver comes from the video backend, and goes when the backend changes: setReceiver(). The local camera's is the source's for good, given to its constructor.

VideoManager says where the source is shown. The primary stream and a stream in a tile change places, role for role, when the tile's stream becomes the one to display: each goes on playing what it plays.

The VideoOutput registers itself: registerVideoOutput() once it is created and again after it moved to another window, unregisterVideoOutput() when it is destroyed (see VideoStreamOutput.qml). The registration outlives receivers, so an output registered before the video backend is ready is attached once a receiver exists. An output that registers as another one leaves takes the stream over from it: the receiver, which decodes only into an output, is never without one in between.
