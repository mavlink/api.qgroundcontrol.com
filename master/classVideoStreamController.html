Detailed Description

One video stream, primary or thermal. Resolves the stream's config from the video settings and the camera's advertised stream, drives the stream's VideoReceiver, and connects the receiver to the QML VideoOutput that displays it.

The VideoOutput registers itself: registerVideoOutput() once it is created and again after it moved to another window, unregisterVideoOutput() when it is destroyed (see FlightDisplayViewVideoOutput.qml). The registration outlives receivers, so an output registered before the video backend is ready is attached once a receiver exists.
